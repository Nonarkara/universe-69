THE OBSERVER'S RECORD

UNIVERSE 69

EDEN is universe 6: a sealed resort where it is always spring. Food, water, and a bed are free. The only scarce things are room and a place to be needed.

LAGOON is universe 9: an island of woods, fish, winters, and predators. Nothing is given.

Watch only. You cannot feed them or step in. They keep living while you are away — a whole day and a night every 12 seconds, about 20 sim-years in a real day.

The light turns: dawn, day, dusk, night. In LAGOON the band comes in to the fire after dark and takes turns keeping watch. EDEN never closes, but it goes to bed.

Come back. An hour away is most of a year in there. Wheel to look closer, drag to pan, click a person to follow, WASD to walk in among them.
