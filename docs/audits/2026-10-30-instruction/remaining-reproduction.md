# Remaining embodied-reproduction gap

This is a controlled diagnostic of existing code, not a natural demographic
result, and no model change was made for it in this instruction increment.

`try_conceive` checks sex, age, pair ID, fertility and pregnancy state but does
not require an actual mating encounter. Its callers supply the eligible cohort
without an encounter gate. A direct `.5` probe used two living paired founders:
father asleep/aboard at(20,20), mother asleep at(140,120), model day1. Forcing
only the conception/miscarriage coins to0/.99 accepted a non-miscarried
pregnancy due on day271 and recorded the distant father's traits.

This establishes a missing causal boundary, not an empirical conception rate.
The next model increment should couple reproductive eligibility to actual
embodied encounters and audit the older grooming/mating helpers that directly
reassign their peer's action. Preserve autonomous choices, childcare, human
biology, the EDEN/LAGOON conditions and the non-intervening observer. Do not
repair demographic outcomes by adjusting fertility coefficients to compensate.

The broader digital-human goal remains incomplete. The current century is a
versioned toy-model experiment, not a validated human population forecast.
