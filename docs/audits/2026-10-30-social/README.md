before activity expiration/reassignment, then resolve contacts among surviving,
non-withdrawn/non-aboard participants. Frozen positions prevent relocated camps
from creating fictitious meetings; frozen sender knowledge prevents immediate

water corners. Transfer does not add body-meter rewards or supplies.

transmission after 90 days and relay at most 3 hops. Reports retain original
observer/seen_day, immediate via sender, heard_day and bounded path. They do
not copy another person's harvest counters; repeat/older reports do not refresh
age, certainty or priority. Current local observation replaces hearsay, and
old incoming reports cannot evict fresher notes at capacity. Newborn/reseeded
people do not inherit these memories; legacy .2 notes receive their direct
observation provenance because .2 had no transmitted notes.
