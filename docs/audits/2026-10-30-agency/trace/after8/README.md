## Measurement boundary

This runner measures `_social_person` **inside the actual scorer**, after the
actor's completed action can be normalized. `prior_entry_action_17` is only a
prior label; it is **not** counted as a causal sleeping block after the fix.
`actual_scoring_*` counters describe actual helper calls/readiness/visible
candidates. Pure extra global queries never supply actor knowledge. Calls and
decision opportunities are repeated observations, not unique people or
independent trials. Completed contact and father/day records are separately
deduplicated. A mother already pregnant can complete contact without accepting
an additional reproductive exposure record.

The historical .7 files one directory above are unchanged. Their entry-readiness
proxy differs from this inside-scoring measurement; compare actual contact and
clinical events, not proxy-readiness percentages. These matched seeds are not
matched human populations: changes alter births, sex cohorts, future random
paths and subsequent choice opportunities.
