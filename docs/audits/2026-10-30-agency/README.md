# Independent choices and protected bodily tasks — engine 2026.10.30.8

This increment repairs decisions that the prior engine could prevent through
its own bookkeeping. It changes future trajectories, including existing saves.
It adds no persistent fields. Clinical reproduction, resources, clock and
technology coefficients are unchanged; no intervention selected survivors.
The configured default remains **100 model years per real day**, four parts per
model day, 360 days per year, eight founder pairs per world.

## Found and changed

- **Finished sleep blocked a fresh choice.** At dawn, sleep duration ended but
  the actor still wore `SLEEP`; its own social-availability query rejected it.
  In the retained .7 seed-69 trace, 5,102 of 5,363 second-generation female
  decisions hit that stale-label veto. Fresh, arrived, completed voluntary
  activity now becomes idle before the actor scores choices. Ongoing sleep,
  unfinished travel, birth, withdrawal and other people's states remain guarded.
- **Biography pointers changed bodily motivation.** Matched bodies/candidates
  could choose MATE versus WORK solely because one had a stored partner pointer.
  The unpaired-only purpose multiplier is removed; free-world ennui applies
  consistently. Both still choose work in appropriate matched conditions.
- **Watch and gathering displaced actual care.** A helper could be reassigned
  to watch or retargeted away from a mother; the prepared mother could also be
  pulled away at night. Care, labour, explicit instruction and other committed
  bodily activities now keep their task and target. Eligible guards and idle
  gathering still operate. Guard selection is not a new voluntary institution.
- **Social activity was universally cut off at 45.** Adult choice/candidate
  eligibility no longer has that upper bound. The separate female clinical
  16–45 gate and all minor/urgency guards remain. This is not a fitted model of
  later-life sexual frequency or male reproductive aging.

## Actual generational contact

Three declared EDEN-only 40-year traces completed with unchanged frozen sources.
Each instrumented/uninstrumented 120-day duplicate had exactly equal full worlds
and events. Every original function is called once; observation adds no RNG draw.
All three reach maternal generation four. Second-generation female clinical
allocations are 67/100/82 for seeds 7/25/69 versus 0/0/0 historically in .7.
These are divergent populations after combined fixes, not one-fix effect sizes
or a fertility calibration. Allocated clinical outcomes, gestation, newborns
observed alive and long-term survival are distinct quantities.

## Remaining objective

This remains an incomplete digital-human model. Local resources, embodied
movement, observed reports, personal procedures, instruction and mutual contact
exist, but physical scale, human time budgets, physiological/social calibration,
open-ended invention and great civilizations remain unverified. The finite
technology ladder ends at writing. No bounded headcount proves viability or a
prediction about people. Historical EDEN decline is not automatically evidence
of Calhoun's mechanism; causes require actual observations at the shipped version.
