## Next causal milestones

1. Passive decision/outcome traces: perceived options, chosen task, obtained
   resources. Prove observation does not change subsequent evolution.
2. Bounded personal experience, learned only by visiting or witnessing. Identical
   needs with different experience should produce different choices; unseen
   information must not leak into memory.
3. Knowledge transmitted by contact, care or teaching instead of appearing
   globally.
4. Controlled comparisons of inherited lessons, including water access, without
   granting resources or guaranteeing survival.

The Architect learns externally. EDEN's controlled conditions do not gain
cross-iteration assistance. A postmortem preventability flag is a model
assumption, not a causal finding. No model behavior changes in this ledger pass.
