## Remaining

illustrative. Locally available materials, fuel, tool stock/wear and ore
processing are abstract; metallurgy has no implemented production economy.
Domestication is coarse enclosure/handling of existing game, without a biological
breeding model or claim that deer are readily domesticable. Language, personal
trust, procedural misinformation, richer planning and general intelligence are
unimplemented. A short or single-seed arc cannot establish long-term cultural
accumulation or a survival benefit. Actual MacBook throughput and millennia
remain unverified. Prior versioned century results remain historical.

Practice opportunities are task-dependent. Completed camp projects do not create
endless free construction practice: an apprentice may need a new worksite.
Awareness alone can therefore fail to become competence. The arc records both,
and historical discoveries can remain after all competent holders disappear.
