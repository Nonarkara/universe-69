# 2D viewer: readable, grounded observation

Task: compare the two worlds, select a real inhabitant, and read identity,
activity and evidence without losing either world. Worlds are the first visual
priority; selected person and activity are second; instruments and records are
third. Applied the anti-slop-design skill and MSH throughout. Root independently
reviewed the resulting phone, tablet and desktop composition and the final
label/value repair; acceptance is scoped to this actual saved packet.

## Found → changed

- Opening EDEN forced LAGOON's otherwise short title to the same height. At
  1024px the old expanded headings were both 77px. Each heading now takes only
  its own content height; LAGOON stays 20.89px when EDEN expands. No clipping or
  smaller type was used to recover its scene.
- Instrument labels/world abbreviations/values were specified at 9/8/10px;
  record text was 10px with 14px lines, and its controls could be 16px high.
  Labels are now 11px, values/record text 12px, record lines 18px and visible
  controls at least 28×28px. The measured pager uses available record height;
  additional content remains reachable in pages.
- The phone clock label wrapped and expanded the masthead to 49.38px. A compact
  label with full accessible meaning brings it to 35px. The question-mark
  control and its focus outline remain inside the viewport. The record counter
  uses one-line `21/21`, with content-sized allocation rather than squeezed type.
- Repeated bundle-age text displaced selected names. Stale/unreachable/unknown
  bundle age remains in one global dated banner; it is not repeated in both
  world titles. Fresh status without a banner remains available as before.
- A card could split `temper` from its percentage. Nonbreaking label/value
  groups now keep purpose, self-control and temper with their values; the
  whole unit wraps when needed, without making the card wider.
- The parenthood flag falsely described a current care action. It now says
  “has a child under 3.” The `breeders` data/chart are preserved but labelled
  **16–45 cohort**, with an accessible explanation of competence/withdrawal,
  both sexes, and why this is not clinical fertility or a viable-pair count.
  Descriptive threshold warnings are labelled alerts, not extinction findings.
- An explicit unknown current yearling ratio reused a historical100% reading.
  Explicit null now stays –; only undefined legacy fields fall back to history.
  Values0/.7, history, charts and model data are preserved.
- Repeated concentric tree circles read as target marks. Deterministic irregular
  layered crowns and restrained material grain keep the existing forest/coast
  inputs, palette, placements and ground cache. No ecological data was invented.
- Any changed record position could make a sleeper glide or an actor cross
  water. Interpolation now requires a recorded WALK/FORAGE/HUNT/SCOUT state
  and, in LAGOON, a known dry chord with diagonal-corner checks. Stationary
  states, missing terrain and rejected gaps cut to the recorded endpoint.
  No target or activity is fabricated. The existing safe-walk easing,
  distance-driven phase, rig, caches, documentary camera and particles remain.

Jost and normal/italic Source Serif 4 now load from the licensed local font
assets. Font provenance and OFL files are in `web/fonts` and `docs/fonts`.
