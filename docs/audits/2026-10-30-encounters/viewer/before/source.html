THE OBSERVER'S RECORD

Universe 69

EDEN is universe 6: a sealed resort where it is always spring. Food, water, and a bed are free. The only scarce things are room and a place to be needed.

LAGOON is universe 9: a Sri Lanka-inspired tropical island of woods, fish, freshwater and predators, shaped by wet and dry seasons. Nothing is given.

Watch only. You cannot feed them or step in. They keep living while you are away — by default, a whole day and a night every 2.4 seconds — 100 simulated years in one real day.

The light turns: dawn, day, dusk, night. In LAGOON the band comes in to the fire after dark and takes turns keeping watch. EDEN never closes, but it goes to bed.

THE FIRST RUN keeps the record of the original two universes: what they did, what we missed, what failed.
